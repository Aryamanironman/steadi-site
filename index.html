Chapter 01 · The body

Shaped for hands that shake.

The story starts with the shell. Ceramic-cream, soft to the touch, gently curved and light enough to lift all dinner long — with a grip that holds on when fingers can't.

  • 1 Soft-touch shell Warm in the hand, wiped clean in seconds.
  • 2 Tactile grip band Fine dots on the flank give unsteady fingers something to find.

Chapter 02 · The opening

Your cutlery. Your table.

No proprietary heads, nothing new to learn: the flexible silicone liner grips any fork, spoon or knife you already own — 6 to 16 millimetres. It lifts out when the meal is done.

Chapter 03 · The steadiness

Two servos, one steady spoon.

A yaw-and-pitch gimbal counters the tremor as it happens — quiet correction, never more than a few degrees, up to 85% steadier. You'd miss it if you didn't watch.

  • 1 Yaw servo Steadies the side-to-side drift of the hand.
  • 2 Pitch servo Levels the lift, so soup arrives as soup.

Chapter 04 · The senses

It feels the tremor, then learns it.

A six-axis sensor reads the hand hundreds of times a second. On the device, a small brain turns that reading into learning — so every meal logged is progress you, your family and your doctor can see.

  • 1 Six-axis IMU Senses motion and tilt at the wrist, where tremor lives.
  • 2 On-device learning Adapts to each user's tremor signature, meal after meal.
  • 3 Reports worth sharing Real sensor data, one tap to a doctor or a family member.

Chapter 05 · The heart

A day of meals on one charge.

Deeper down, a small battery keeps the heart beating. One button, two presses: a short press turns it on, a long press changes mode. The little light keeps count.

  • 1 Mode / power button Short press on / off · long press mode change.
  • 2 Indicator light Shows battery level and mode status at a glance.
  • 3 Battery + clean power A full day of meals per charge, with a regulator keeping the current steady.

Chapter 06 · Together again

Rest it in the dock.

Every part returns where it belongs. A deep pocket holds the handle overnight; a small light keeps watch on the charge. By morning, it's ready to do it all again — no setup, no fuss, no help needed.
